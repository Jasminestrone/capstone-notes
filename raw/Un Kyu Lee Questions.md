## My Project — 30 Second Pitch
Gen Z is the least comfortable generation with their screen time, yet restriction-based solutions — app blockers, screen time limits, nudge timers — have consistently failed. That's because they treat the symptom, not the cause: 80% of Gen Z says FOMO and lack of motivation are what keep them on their phones, and you can't block that away.

My project is rooted in the neuroscience of boredom. When you're not doing anything, your brain activates the default mode network — the system responsible for self-reflection, creativity, and life planning. Modern smartphones have essentially shut the DMN off by eliminating boredom entirely. The consequences are showing up as rising depression, anxiety, and a pervasive lack of purpose in Gen Z.

My answer isn't restriction — it's reclamation. I'm building a purposefully boring open-source micro-gadget, grounded in DMN research, designed to make doing nothing feel worth doing. Devices like the Micro Journal are the closest existing precedent, but they still give you something to do. Mine is trying to give you nothing — and make that compelling.

## Introduction
- Tech relationship is unsustainable, people feel loss of control over their own attention (Newport, 2019)
- Gen Z hit hardest — only 14% comfortable with their screen time (ExpressVPN survey)
- Frame the project: not about restricting phone use, but about reclaiming boredom

# Key Themes
### **The Neuroscience of Boredom**
- Boredom triggers the default mode network (DMN) - activates when you have nothing else to think about (Brooks, 2025; Wilson et al., 2014)
- DMN drives introspection, self-reflection, long-term life planning
- Engaging with boredom is essential for developing a sense of purpose and meaning (Brooks, 2025)

### **The Elimination of Boredom**
- TikTok, Reels, Shorts have "cured" boredom with infinite on-demand stimulation
- 83% of respondents spent zero time "relaxing or thinking" in the past 24 hours (ExpressVPN survey)
- DMN is being shut off almost entirely for a generation
- 80% of Gen Z cite FOMO and lack of motivation as why they can't stop (ExpressVPN survey)
- 68% feel anxious when separated from their phones (TechCabal, 2024)
- Harvard study: subjects would rather shock themselves than sit with their thoughts for 15 minutes (Wilson et al., 2014)

### **The Consequences**
- Gen Z has the least sense of what they want to do with their lives
- Researchers link this directly to rise in depression and anxiety (Brooks, 2025)
- 7+ hours of daily screen time doubles depression/anxiety risk (San Diego State University study)
- Constant stimulation prevents the DMN-driven introspection that historically helped people figure out their purpose

### **Existing Responses and Why They Fall Short**
- Newport: willpower-based tips and screen time apps are insufficient, need a full philosophy of technology use (Newport, 2019)
- Every major solution focuses on restriction — blocking apps, setting timers, guilt
- None address underlying FOMO/motivation problem
- 28% of Gen Z actively struggling to cut back, 12% have given up entirely (ExpressVPN survey)
- Dual-mode social media interface research — even academic solutions focus on changing the feed, not the behavior (arxiv, 2024)
- Microjournal: open source writerDeck, distraction-free, multiple form factors (microjournal GitHub)
- Xteink X4: e-ink phone, deliberate limitation
- Tangara: open source music player
- Growing "boring gadget" movement — but none are built around the neuroscience of why boredom itself matters
# Synthesis + Conclusions
- Boredom is not something to be cured, it's something essential to reclaim
- DMN needs space to operate
- Restriction alone doesn't work
- The gap: no device offers a compelling, neuroscience-grounded replacement that makes "doing nothing" feel worth doing
- Make the case for the project

## Microjournal
Microjournal is an open source open hardware journal, different itterations contain differieng levels of portability and different sizing of keyboards, from 40% ortholinear to a full size 65% ISO keyboard, folding and not folding. It is a genre of [writerDecks](https://www.writerdeck.org/) , or small journals meant to be distraction free, as in the modern day many of our writing devices are also our video watching devices and allow for us to get sidetracked incredibly easily

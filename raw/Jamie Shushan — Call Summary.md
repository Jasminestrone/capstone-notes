## What She's Seen

### Pre vs. Post-COVID
- Phone impact on students was visible **before COVID** but became dramatically more pronounced after
- In earlier years, not all students had phones on them — that created natural space for connection
- COVID cohorts had less in-person time; those groups struggled noticeably more to bond outside structured activities

### Phones as a Barrier to Community
- The program depends on students connecting **authentically outside of planned activities** — phones disrupted exactly that
- When phones were allowed for a year or two, it created a **barrier to communication** and stunted relationship formation
- Key loss: the ability to overcome social nerves and meet new people, which used to happen naturally in downtime

### Independent Thinking
- From an education standpoint, students now struggle more to **work or think independently**
- Need more hand-holding on assignments
- Before COVID there was more natural "space for thinking" — space to be bored — and that space produced real things: connecting with someone, thinking about something you care about

### On Boredom Specifically
- She sees that **"there is no more time to just be"** and is genuinely worried about it
- Hadn't previously thought about a device designed to bring the brain back to a state of boredom/DMN activation — found the framing interesting

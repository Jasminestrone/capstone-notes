Quick-start tips

  • Tap Diary in the nav the moment you feel bored.
  • Fill in the next empty entry as it's happening.
  • Your progress saves automatically to this device.
  • Use Export at the bottom to send your data.

How to use this diary

  • 1
    Carry it with youKeep this page open (bookmark it) during the 3–5 day study period.
  • 2
    Capture the momentThe instant you feel bored, open the Diary tab and fill in the next empty entry in real time — not from memory later.
  • 3
    Be honestThere are no right or wrong answers. Your genuine experience is exactly what matters.
  • 4
    Up to 3 entries per dayEach day has 3 entry slots. If you feel bored more than 3 times, use the Extra entry at the bottom of that day.
  • 5
    Missed a day?Tick the "didn't feel bored" box or add a brief note at the bottom of that day's section.
  • 6
    Reflect at the endAfter your last day, go to the Reflect tab and answer the short reflection questions.
  • 7
    Export & returnUse the Export button to download your data as a text file, then share it with the researcher.

What each question asks

Time & Duration When it started and how long it lasted.
How bored? Intensity: 1 = barely bored, 5 = intensely bored.
Where? The physical space you're in.
Who with? Your social situation at that moment.
Trigger The activity or situation that brought on boredom.
Response What you instinctively did or felt like doing.
Thoughts/Feelings Any observations, emotions, or wishes worth noting.

End-of-Study Reflection

Complete this after your last diary day. Take your time!

1. Which situation made you feel most bored during the study?
2. Did anything surprise you about when or how often you felt bored?
3. What did you usually do to cope with boredom — and did it help?
4. Do you see a pattern in where or when your boredom tends to happen?
5. Anything else you'd like to share with the researcher?

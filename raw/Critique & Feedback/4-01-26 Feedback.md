**Participant Selection**
- [ ] Once survey responses come in, review demographic data and responses before scheduling anyone
- [ ] Map respondents to rough personas — identify extremes (youngest/oldest, healthiest/unhealthiest phone relationship)
- [ ] Select interview participants based on range, not just availability

**Interview Timeline**
- [ ] Set a hard deadline for when interviews will be done and synthesis begins
